## Task 2. The spec — Roster Tagging Confidence

### Outcome
Reduce wrong-kid clips in personal reels and raise reel *open* rate by stopping wrong-kid thumbnails from shipping. Success = fewer tagging-related support tickets, fewer reels held/unopened due to identity errors, coach confirm time under ~60s per game for typical ambiguity volume.

### Problem
Jersey OCR / roster matching confuses look-alike numbers (4↔14, 1↔11, digit swaps). Wrong tags produce wrong personal reels and wrong thumbnails → parents don’t open → share metrics look fine while trust erodes.

### In scope
1. **Confidence score** on each jersey→roster match (model + heuristics for look-alike pairs).  
2. **Look-alike watchlist** seeded with known confusions (1/11, 4/14, 6/9, 3/8, digit-swap pairs); club-specific additions from coach corrections.  
3. **Coach Confirm queue**: before family send, surface only low-confidence / look-alike conflicts (not every clip).  
4. **Hold policy**: personal reels for affected players stay unpublished until confirm or exclude; high-confidence reels still send.  
5. **Training loop**: coach Confirm / Reassign / Exclude becomes labeled data.  
6. **Thumbnail regenerate** after a reassignment before notify.

### Out of scope
- Full livestream / broadcast  
- Parent Reel Editor expansion (trim/music)  
- Visual redesign, multi-sport app icon  
- Replacing coach upload with new camera hardware  
- Auto-sending low-confidence reels with a disclaimer

### Human in the loop
**Coach** confirms or reassigns ambiguous tags before family delivery. Product/ML does not silently guess on look-alike pairs below threshold. Support can see confirm history on tickets.

### Acceptance criteria (testable)
1. Given a clip whose top two jersey hypotheses are a look-alike pair both on the roster (e.g. 4 and 14) with confidence gap < threshold, the clip appears in Coach Confirm and is **not** included in either personal reel until Confirm/Reassign/Exclude.  
2. Confirming “Ava T. (#14)” places the clip only in Ava’s reel and regenerates Ava’s thumbnail within the send pipeline.  
3. Games with zero low-confidence tags show **no** Confirm step; auto-send path unchanged.  
4. Coach can complete a 3-item queue in ≤60 seconds in usability test (median).  
5. Tagging-related support tickets (wrong kid / wrong number) drop ≥50% for pilot clubs in 30 days vs prior 30.  
6. Analytics: share rate denominator documentation updated; dashboard shows open rate of generated reels (including never-opened), not only share-among-opened.

### Top two failure modes after ship
| Failure | What goes wrong | How we catch it |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1. Threshold too aggressive | Coaches flooded with confirms → skip / abandon → delayed or unsent reels | Monitor confirms-per-game, abandon rate, time-to-send; auto-loosen threshold if median confirms > N |
| 2. Threshold too loose / wrong watchlist | Bad tags still ship; Ridgeline-class tickets continue | Ticket tag “wrong player”; weekly wrong-tag rate; sample audit of sent reels with look-alike jerseys on roster |

### Non-goals / risks
Coaches may be offline after games — need clear push + “reels waiting on 3 confirms” state. Do not block the whole team’s recap on one player’s ambiguity if product allows team auto-recap with face-blurred/unassigned clips; personal reels still hold.
